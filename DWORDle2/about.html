DWORDle 2 について

DWORDle 2 は、答えが2つ・盤面が1つの新感覚ワードパズルです。前作「DWORDle」の出題互換性を維持しつつ、演出、履歴、実績、分析、難易度別問題、EXTRA SHOTなどを追加した続編です。

About DWORDle 2

DWORDle 2 is a word puzzle with two answers on one board. It is a sequel that preserves puzzle compatibility with the original DWORDle while adding new presentation, history, achievements, analysis, difficulty bands, EXTRA SHOT, and more.

Concept, game design, development, and operation: kakira9618
Contact: kurokuro917@gmail.com
