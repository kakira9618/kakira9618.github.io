3. Google アナリティクス（任意）

品質改善と利用傾向の把握のため Google Analytics 4を使用します。ただし、Google タグは利用者が明示的に「同意する」または「計測を許可」を選んだ後にだけ読み込みます。拒否時は、同意状態を含め Google へ送信しません（Basic Consent Mode）。ブラウザが Do Not Track を送る場合も読み込みません。

同意後に送信する内容は、画面名、パラメータを除いた本サイトの画面 URL、ゲームモード、勝敗、手数、デイリー問題かどうかです。通信に伴い、Google は IP アドレス、ユーザーエージェント、時刻、参照元、Google が生成するクライアントID・セッション情報、国・都市程度の概算地域、ブラウザ・端末情報等を処理する場合があります。プレイヤー名・ID、入力単語、問題の答え、プレイ履歴、端末内アクティビティは送信しません。

利用目的は、画面・機能の利用傾向やゲームバランスを集計し、ゲームを改善することです。利用者単位の広告、販売、プロファイリング、自動的な意思決定には使用しません。適用法上必要な場合の処理根拠は利用者の同意です。

現在の GA4 管理設定

  • 拡張計測: 無効
  • Google シグナル: OFF
  • 広告パーソナライズ: OFF
  • Google 広告とのリンク: なし
  • データ保持: イベントデータ 2か月／ユーザーデータ 14か月

保持期間の設定は、GA4 のユーザー単位・イベント単位の詳細データに適用されます。Google の標準集計レポート等には同じ形で適用されない場合があります。

Privacy Policy and External Transfer Notice

Effective and last updated: July 26, 2026

At a glance
  • All game features work without consenting to Google Analytics.
  • No Google tag is loaded and no data is sent to Google until you consent.
  • Your play history, typed words, answers, player name, and player ID are not sent to Google Analytics.
  • You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Data.

1. Operator and scope

This policy applies to DWORDle 2, a web game provided by @kakira9618 (the “Operator”). Contact the Operator at kurokuro917@gmail.com or via X (@kakira9618).

2. Data stored on your device

Current games, play history and statistics, achievements and unlocks, tutorial state, preferences, player-card name and ID, local activity, and your analytics choice are stored in browser localStorage. They support game continuity and are not sent to the Operator’s server or Google Analytics. They remain until you use Settings → Data → Delete all data, clear site data in your browser, or browser storage is otherwise removed.

History exports, result images, and share text are created only at your request. Data you choose to save or share is then governed by the destination you select.

3. Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is used to understand aggregate usage and improve the game. The Google tag is loaded only after you explicitly accept or select “Allow analytics.” If you decline, nothing—including the refusal signal—is sent to Google (Basic Consent Mode). It is also not loaded when your browser sends Do Not Track.

After consent, DWORDle 2 sends screen names, a sanitized site URL without route parameters, game mode, result, number of guesses, and whether a puzzle is daily. Google may also process request metadata such as IP address, user agent, time, and referrer; Google-generated client and session identifiers; approximate country/city; and browser or device information. Player name or ID, typed words, puzzle answers, play history, and local activity are never included in analytics events.

The current GA4 configuration has Enhanced Measurement, Google Signals, ad personalization, and unnecessary Google data sharing disabled; it has no Google Ads link. Event-data retention is set to two months and user-data retention to 14 months. Google’s data processing terms have been reviewed. Retention controls apply to user- and event-level data and may not apply in the same way to standard aggregated reports.

With consent, GA4 may set _ga and _ga_<container-id> first-party cookies to distinguish users and preserve session state. Google lists a default duration of two years, subject to shorter browser limits. Selecting Settings → Data → Stop analytics stops future game events and attempts to delete these analytics cookies.

4. External transfers

  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages): connection information such as IP address, requested URL, time, and user agent is processed as necessary to deliver and secure the site.
  • Google LLC (Google Analytics): the usage and connection data described above is sent only after consent, for aggregate usage measurement.

These providers may process data outside your country. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement, Google Privacy Policy, and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Your choices and rights

You may decline or withdraw analytics consent without losing game functionality. You can view, export, or delete locally stored game data in the app. Contact the Operator for requests available under applicable data-protection law. A request may not be fulfillable where the data cannot be identified or is not held by the Operator. Minors should consult a guardian where appropriate before consenting; declining does not change gameplay.

6. Security and changes

Analytics fields are kept to the minimum described above, and typed words are excluded by design. The date on this page will be updated when this policy changes. Material changes to collection purposes or recipients will prompt renewed consent where required.
